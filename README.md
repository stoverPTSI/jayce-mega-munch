# 🍪 Jayce's Snack Store Mega Munch! 🦖

A custom 2.5D *Hole.io* / *Katamari*-style web game built for **Jayce** and his Grandpa!

## 🎮 Features
- **Custom Cartoon Jayce**: Modeled after Jayce's photo with his signature sandy-blonde bangs, big toothy grin (that opens wide to **CHOMP!** near snacks), and his heather-gray & blue `"J"` shirt — plus selectable **Super Blue Hero** and **Blue Dino Suit** outfits!
- **Jayce's Favorite Treats**: Gobble up **Chocolate Chip Cookies** 🍪, **Peanut Butter Cups** 🥜, **Breakfast Sausage Links** 🌭, **Dr. Seuss Green Eggs** 🍳, and **Blue Lightning Turbo Treats** ⚡!
- **Roaming Rival Dinosaurs**: Watch out for bigger dinosaurs with **Red Rings** (`⚠️`), or eat snacks to outgrow them until their rings turn **Green** (`😋`) and swallow the dinosaurs whole!
- **3-Strike Lives System**: Jayce keeps 100% of his size when chomped and gets 3 lives (`❤️❤️❤️`) per game.
- **Walk to the Snack Store**: Grow through 6 size levels to gobble up the entire **Jayce & Grandpa's Corner Snack Store** 🏪 while Grandpa cheers you on!

## 🚀 How to Play Locally
Open `index.html` directly in any modern browser, or start a local web server:
```bash
python3 -m http.server 8900
```
Then open **http://localhost:8900** in your browser.
